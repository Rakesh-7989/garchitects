## The Master Plan is Everything

In a gated community, individual homes matter — but the master plan matters more. A well-considered master plan determines traffic flow, pedestrian safety, green coverage, privacy between homes, and the placement of amenities. Get this wrong and no amount of beautiful villas can compensate.

At G Architects, the master plan is always the first deliverable — before a single villa elevation is designed.

## Green Space is Not a Luxury

Projects that minimise open green space in favour of more sellable area may appear commercially smart in the short term. But buyers increasingly recognise that a community with generous parks, jogging tracks, and tree-lined pathways offers a fundamentally better quality of life — and holds its value far better over time.

Our SIRI VILLAS project in Kardhanoor dedicates a significant portion of the 6-acre site to green corridors, open gymnasiums, a children's play zone, and a central park — because these are the spaces residents will use every single day.

## Connectivity Within the Community

Internal roads must be wide enough for two vehicles to pass comfortably, with dedicated pedestrian paths that do not share the driving lane. Lighting, signage, and clear wayfinding are often overlooked in smaller communities — but they define the lived experience after handover.

## Amenities That Residents Actually Use

Swimming pools, badminton courts, and clubhouses are expected — but the most valued amenities are often the simple ones: a shaded seating area near the park, a small community garden, a covered carpark that keeps vehicles protected from rain. Understanding how residents actually live — not just what looks impressive on a brochure — is the foundation of great amenity design.

## Consistent Elevation Vocabulary

In a gated community, individual villas should feel distinct enough to express the owner's identity, yet cohesive enough to create a unified street-scape. A consistent elevation vocabulary — shared materials, proportions, and design language — is what separates a premium community from a fragmented collection of buildings.
