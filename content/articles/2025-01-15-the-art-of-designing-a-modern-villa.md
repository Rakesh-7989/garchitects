## The Starting Point: Site & Context

Every great villa begins with the land it sits on. Before a single line is drawn, we spend time understanding the plot — its orientation, the direction of prevailing winds, the path of sunlight through the seasons, and the views worth capturing or screening.

In Hyderabad's climate, east-facing living areas catch the gentler morning sun, while deep overhangs on the west protect interiors from the harsh afternoon glare. These are not stylistic choices — they are the foundation of a home that actually *works*.

## Spatial Flow Over Room Count

The modern villa is not defined by the number of rooms. It is defined by how space flows — from a compressed entryway that suddenly opens to a double-height living volume, to the way the kitchen connects to the garden through a frameless glass door.

We design sequences of experience, not just square footage.

## Material Honesty

At G Architects, we believe materials should be allowed to be themselves. Exposed concrete reveals its texture. Teak shows its grain. Stone is laid to emphasise its natural strata. This honesty gives a home its character — something no amount of ornament can replicate.

## The Landscape as the Fifth Facade

Most architects stop at four walls and a roof. We consider the landscape the fifth façade. The garden, the pathway, the pool edge — these are extensions of the architecture, designed with the same rigour as the interior.
